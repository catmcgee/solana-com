# Solana Documentation: Assets

> Every page is available as raw Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL
> (the links here already include it). The site-wide index is at
> https://solana.com/llms.txt.

- [Assets](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets.md): Create and manage tokens: tokenized funds and securities, stablecoins, points, NFTs and game items.
- [Let players own items across games](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets/games.md): Mint an item to a player and read it from a second app. Guides for game items, NFTs and game state on Solana.
- [Issue a tokenized asset with compliance controls](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets/issue-asset.md): Create a Token-2022 asset, such as a fund share or real-world asset, with metadata, an approval list, and a pause switch enforced on every transfer.
- [Read the asset code](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets/issue-asset/browser.md): Read the code that creates a Token-2022 mint with metadata, a pause switch, a permanent delegate and default-frozen accounts, and step through its output.
- [Go live with your asset](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets/issue-asset/go-live.md): Before you issue on mainnet: move the asset's authorities to a multisig, approve holders from KYC attestations, and write the pause and recovery runbook.
- [Run it from your machine](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets/issue-asset/local.md): Create the restricted asset from your terminal on Surfpool or devnet, approve holders, and test the blocked transfer, pause, and recovery yourself.
- [Hold a restricted asset](https://solana.com/developers/docs/assets/issue-asset/try-it.md): See a restricted asset at work: two investors are approved, a transfer to one succeeds, and a transfer to an unapproved account fails onchain.
