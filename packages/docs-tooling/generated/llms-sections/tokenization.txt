# Solana Documentation: tokenization

> Issuing stablecoins, tokenizing real-world assets, and operating compliant tokens on Solana.

> Every page is available as raw Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL
> (the links here already include it). The site-wide index is at
> https://solana.com/llms.txt.

- [Compliance](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance.md): Evidence that a compliance check happened without putting personal data onchain, see what Confidential Balances hide, and check which issuer controls still work with encrypted amounts.
- [Attestation-based compliance](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/attestation-based-compliance.md): Attest that a KYC, sanctions, or travel-rule check happened without putting personal data onchain, and wire the attestation to the token's gate.
- [Privacy and transfer controls](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/compliance/privacy-and-transfer-controls.md): What Confidential Balances hide, which issuer controls still work when amounts are encrypted, and how to give an auditor visibility.
- [Custody](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/custody.md): How a gated token meets a custodian, how issuer and transfer agent duties divide onchain, and what an asset manager's vault needs before it can hold the token.
- [Custody integration](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/custody-integration.md): How a gated token meets a custodian: the custody models available, and what a custodian must support to hold your asset.
- [Transfer agent responsibilities](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/transfer-agent-responsibilities.md): How issuer and transfer agent duties divide when the register of record is onchain: who signs mint and burn, who keeps the holder list, and how the two books are reconciled.
- [Vault architectures](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/custody/vault-architectures.md): How an asset manager's vault holds a gated token, synchronous versus request-and-settle designs, and why funds priced at a periodic valuation point need the request-and-settle design.
- [Token design](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/design.md): Decide whether the token is the asset itself or a record of one held elsewhere, which controls follow from the asset type, and the operating duties each control brings.
- [Choosing token controls](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/design/choosing-controls.md): How to choose between frozen-by-default accounts with Token ACL, transfer hooks, and manual freeze and thaw, and how to split token authorities so no single key can do everything.
- [Deployment options](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/design/deployment-options.md): Where a tokenized asset should live, from public mainnet to Confidential Balances, and what Solana Privacy Rings and Private Channels will change for issuers once they are ready for production.
- [Program and authority design](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/design/program-and-authority-design.md): Choose between SPL Token, Token-2022, and a custom program, then decide which authorities sit on a PDA, an onchain multisig, or a single key.
- [Delivery vs Payment (DvP) using delegation](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/dvp.md): Settle commercial paper and other securities against USDC atomically (delivery vs payment) with Token-2022 extensions, without writing a program.
- [NAV Strikes for Money Market Funds on Solana](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/nav-strikes.md): Run multiple daily NAV strikes for a money market fund with Token-2022 extensions, with atomic subscription and redemption.
- [Securities](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/securities.md): What is specific to tokenizing a security on Solana, from the register of record and the transfer agent's role to transfer restrictions, corporate actions, and delivery versus payment.
- [Stablecoins](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/stablecoins.md): What is specific to issuing a stablecoin on Solana, from supply tied to a reserve to the controls that matter for a token used as the cash side of other people's payments and trades.
- [Permissioned tokens with Token ACL (sRFC37)](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/token-acl.md): Issue permissioned tokens on Solana with Token ACL and the ABL Gate program: allow and block lists that the network enforces.
- [Tokenized funds](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tokenized-funds.md): What is specific to issuing fund shares on Solana, from a price struck at a valuation point to request-and-settle subscriptions and redemptions and the controls a share register needs.
- [Tokenization tutorials](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials.md): Build one gated Token-2022 mint, then take the other sides of it, the custodian holding it for a client and an asset manager's vault, with the Mosaic SDK, Kit, Web3.js v3, or Rust.
- [Hold a gated token as a custodian](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/custodian.md): Take the custodian side of a gated token: derive the custody address, get it allowlisted, receive the asset, observe a freeze, and reconcile at a stated commitment level.
- [Issue a gated token](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/issuing.md): Build a Token-2022 mint whose accounts start frozen, gate who can hold it with an onchain allowlist, and run the controls an issuer operates day to day.
- [Create the mint](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/issuing/create-the-mint.md): Create a Token-2022 mint for a tokenized security with frozen-by-default accounts, pause, a permanent delegate, permissioned burn, and an onchain allowlist, with each operational authority on its own key.
- [Gate the holders](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/issuing/gate-the-holders.md): Put an address on an onchain allowlist so its token account can be thawed through the gate, and confirm that an address which is not on the list cannot hold the token.
- [Operate the token](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/issuing/operate-the-token.md): Run the controls an issuer operates day to day on a gated token: freeze one account, pause the token, force a transfer, burn with and without the holder, and offboard a wallet.
- [Prepare a vault for a gated token](https://solana.com/developers/docs/tokenization/tutorials/vault.md): Prepare what an asset manager's vault needs before it can hold a gated token: show that the gate blocks an unlisted vault, allowlist it, and confirm what a pause does to it.
