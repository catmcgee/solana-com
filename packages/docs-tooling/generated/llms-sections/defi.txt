# Solana Documentation: Markets

> Trading systems, DEX and oracle integration, and financial applications on Solana.

> Every page is available as raw Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL
> (the links here already include it). The site-wide index is at
> https://solana.com/llms.txt.

- [Markets](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi.md): Build trading systems, integrate DEXs and oracles, and run financial applications on Solana.
- [Choosing the cash leg](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/cash-leg.md): How a tokenized asset settles against its payment on Solana, from atomic exchange in one transaction to how the cash instrument and the mint's controls constrain each other.
- [Confidential settlement](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/confidential-settlement.md): What Confidential Balances, Solana Privacy Rings, and Private Channels keep private when you settle a trade, who can audit it, and what DvP supports today.
- [Delivery vs Payment (DvP) program](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/dvp-program.md): The Solana Foundation's DvP program holds an asset leg and a cash leg in program-owned escrow until a settlement authority named in the trade settles both in one transaction. Covers the program ID, roles, account layout, errors, supported tokens, and step-by-step guides in TypeScript and Rust.
- [Create a Trade](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/dvp-program/create-a-trade.md): Set up the DvP program clients, derive the trade record and its two escrow accounts, and record the terms of a trade with CreateDvp.
- [Fund the Legs](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/dvp-program/fund-the-legs.md): Verify the stored terms of a DvP trade, then fund a leg with a plain TransferChecked into its escrow account.
- [Settle the Trade](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/dvp-program/settle-the-trade.md): The settlement authority re-checks the stored terms, creates the four recipient token accounts, and sends SettleDvp to move both legs of a funded trade in one transaction, then waits for finalized before recording it.
- [Unwind a Trade](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/dvp-program/unwind-a-trade.md): Take a leg back with Reclaim, unwind a trade with Cancel or Reject, and recover a deposit that landed after the trade closed with Recover.
- [Add Solana to Your Exchange](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/exchange.md): Learn how to integrate Solana into your exchange with this guide covering node setup and implementation details for deposits and withdrawals.
- [Settlement and finality](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/finality.md): What processed, confirmed, and finalized mean on Solana, how long each takes, and which commitment level a system of record should treat as settled.
- [MEV Protection with Jito DontFront](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/mev-protection.md): Protect Solana transactions from sandwich attacks with Jito's dontfront feature: how it works, bundle ordering rules, tips, and TypeScript and Rust examples.
- [Settle a trade atomically (DvP)](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/settle-trade.md): Price a trade from a live Pyth oracle, then settle it with the DvP program: the asset and the USDC payment move in one transaction, or neither moves.
- [Read the trade code](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/settle-trade/browser.md): Read the code that prices a trade from a Pyth feed, records it with the DvP program, and settles both legs in one transaction, then step through its output.
- [Go live with trade settlement](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/settle-trade/go-live.md): Before you settle real trades: the mainnet values, who holds the settlement key, which price checks to keep, and when a trade counts as settled.
- [Run it from your machine](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/settle-trade/local.md): Add the trade to your Issue an asset project, generate the DvP client, and price and settle a trade from your terminal on a local Surfpool network or devnet.
- [Watch a trade settle](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/settle-trade/try-it.md): See a DvP trade at work: the price comes from a live Pyth feed, settlement fails with the cash leg missing, then both legs move in one transaction.
- [A Guide to Stake-weighted Quality of Service on Solana](https://solana.com/developers/docs/defi/stake-weighted-qos.md): Stake-weighted quality of service (QoS) lets leaders prioritize transactions sent through a staked validator, which helps resist Sybil attacks.
