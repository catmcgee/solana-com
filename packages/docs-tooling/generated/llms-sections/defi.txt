# Solana Documentation: Markets & Trading

> Trading systems, DEX and oracle integration, and financial applications on Solana.

> Every page is available as raw Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL
> (the links here already include it). The site-wide index is at
> https://solana.com/llms.txt.

- [Markets & Trading](https://solana.com/docs/defi.md): Build trading systems, integrate DEXs and oracles, and run financial applications on the chain where DeFi settles in milliseconds for fractions of a cent.
- [Delivery vs Payment (DvP) program](https://solana.com/docs/defi/dvp-program.md): The Solana Foundation's DvP program holds an asset leg and a cash leg in program-owned escrow until a settlement authority named in the trade settles both in one transaction. Covers the program ID, roles, account layout, errors, supported tokens, and step-by-step guides in TypeScript and Rust.
- [Create a Trade](https://solana.com/docs/defi/dvp-program/create-a-trade.md): Set up the DvP program clients, derive the trade record and its two escrow accounts, and record the terms of a trade with CreateDvp.
- [Fund the Legs](https://solana.com/docs/defi/dvp-program/fund-the-legs.md): Verify the stored terms of a DvP trade, then fund a leg with a plain TransferChecked into its escrow account.
- [Settle the Trade](https://solana.com/docs/defi/dvp-program/settle-the-trade.md): The settlement authority re-checks the stored terms, creates the four recipient token accounts, and sends SettleDvp to move both legs of a funded trade in one transaction, then waits for finalized before recording it.
- [Unwind a Trade](https://solana.com/docs/defi/dvp-program/unwind-a-trade.md): Take a leg back with Reclaim, unwind a trade with Cancel or Reject, and recover a deposit that landed after the trade closed with Recover.
- [Add Solana to Your Exchange](https://solana.com/docs/defi/exchange.md): Learn how to integrate Solana into your exchange with this guide covering node setup and implementation details for deposits and withdrawals.
- [MEV Protection with Jito DontFront](https://solana.com/docs/defi/mev-protection.md): Learn how to protect your Solana transactions from sandwich attacks using Jito's dontfront feature. This guide covers how sandwich attacks work, how dontfront prevents front-running, bundle ordering rules, tip strategies, and integration examples in TypeScript and Rust.
- [A Guide to Stake-weighted Quality of Service on Solana](https://solana.com/docs/defi/stake-weighted-qos.md): Stake-weighted QoS (Quality-of-Service) is an implementation feature which, when enabled, allows leaders (block producers) to identify and prioritize transactions proxied through a staked validator as an additional sybil resistance mechanism.
