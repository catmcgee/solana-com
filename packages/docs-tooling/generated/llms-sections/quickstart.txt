# Solana Documentation: Quickstart

> Every page is available as raw Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL
> (the links here already include it). The site-wide index is at
> https://solana.com/llms.txt.

- [Quickstart](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart.md): Send dollars, then create an asset, send it to someone, and freeze their account, on devnet in about 8 minutes. No Solana experience needed.
- [Check a balance](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/check-balance.md): Create a wallet, read its balance from devnet, and get free test SOL and test dollars (USDC).
- [Create an asset](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/create-asset.md): Create an asset, like a share or a currency, with its name and symbol stored onchain, and mint 1,000 units to yourself, in one transaction.
- [Freeze a holder](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/freeze-holder.md): Freeze a holder's token account so the network blocks their transfers, then thaw it.
- [Send the asset](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/send-asset.md): Send 100 units of your asset to a new holder, opening their token account in the same transaction.
- [Send dollars](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/send-dollars.md): Send 1 USDC (test dollars on devnet) to a new address: your first transaction.
