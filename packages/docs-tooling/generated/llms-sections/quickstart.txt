# Solana Documentation: Quickstart

> Every page is available as raw Markdown by appending `.md` to its URL
> (the links here already include it). The site-wide index is at
> https://solana.com/llms.txt.

- [Quickstart](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart.md): Check a balance, send SOL, and create a token on devnet in about 5 minutes. No Solana experience needed.
- [Check a balance](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/check-balance.md): Create a wallet, read its balance from devnet, and get free test SOL.
- [Create a token](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/create-token.md): Create a token, open an account for it, and mint 1,000 of it, in one transaction.
- [Send SOL](https://solana.com/developers/docs/quickstart/send-sol.md): Build, sign, and send your first transaction: 0.01 SOL to a new address.
