Create a token for a fund share, a security or a real-world asset. Its rules
live in the token itself: holders must be approved before they can receive it,
the issuer can pause every transfer, and the issuer can recover tokens from any
account. The Token-2022 program checks these rules on every transfer, in every
wallet and app.

The path takes about 45 minutes and follows the life of one demo asset, the
Lisbon Solar Fund (LSF):

<ol className="sol-flow not-prose" aria-hidden="true"><li><b>Define</b><span>name, rules</span></li><li><b>Approve</b><span>who may hold it</span></li><li><b>Issue</b><span>mint to holders</span></li><li><b>Transfer</b><span>under your rules</span></li><li><b>Control</b><span>pause · recover</span></li></ol>

1. **Define** the asset: name, symbol, metadata and rules.
2. **Approve** who may hold it, after your KYC check.
3. **Issue** it by minting to approved holders.
4. **Transfer** it between holders, under your rules.
5. **Control** it: pause every transfer, or move tokens out of an account.

Every code block on these pages is a tested example. Its output was recorded on
a local [Surfpool](/docs/tools/surfpool) network.

## Is this the right tool?

|                                  | Spreadsheet or transfer-agent database | Token on Solana (Token-2022)                   |
| -------------------------------- | -------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Who keeps the register           | You, reconciled with every partner     | One shared ledger that everyone reads          |
| When transfers settle            | Business hours, T+1 or T+2             | Any time, confirmed in under a second          |
| Where the rules are enforced     | Your app and back office               | The Token-2022 program, on every transfer      |
| Wallet and exchange support      | A custom integration each time         | Apps that support Token-2022; many venues won't list a restricted token |

<Columns cols={2}>
<Card title="A good fit if" icon="check" color="#16a34a">
- Holders sit outside your company and transfer between themselves.
- Your rules (who can hold it, pauses, recovery) must apply everywhere the asset goes.
- Settlement time or reconciliation cost is a real problem for you.
</Card>
<Card title="Not the right fit if" icon="x" color="#dc2626">
- All holdings stay inside your company. A database is simpler.
- Balances must be hidden from the public. Read about [confidential balances](/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer) first.
- You don't have the legal roles covered (issuer, transfer agent, licences). Talk to a partner first.
</Card>
</Columns>
