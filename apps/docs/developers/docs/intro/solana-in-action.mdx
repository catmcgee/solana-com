<div className="sol-inaction-explain">

<p className="sol-inaction-kicker">What Solana is doing</p>

<div className="sol-inaction-flow" aria-hidden="true">
<span>Arrow key</span><i />
<span>Transaction</span><i />
<span className="is-code">2048 program</span><i />
<span>Your game account</span>
</div>

<ol className="sol-inaction-steps">
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">Play opens a game account and a session key</p>
<p>Your board and score live in a small account that the 2048 program owns. Play also creates a session key: a short-lived key, funded with a little devnet SOL, that the program accepts for moves, so you don't approve every one.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">Each arrow key sends a transaction</p>
<p>The transaction holds one instruction, `push_in_direction`, with the direction you pressed. The session key signs it and pays the fee, a fraction of a cent.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The program updates your board</p>
<p>The 2048 program merges the tiles and writes the new board and score to your game account, and updates the high-score list. It all happens in one transaction: every change, or none.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The game shows the move before it's final</p>
<p>To feel instant, the game draws the result as soon as a validator processes the transaction, and rolls the move back if the network doesn't confirm it.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p className="sol-inaction-links">[Program source](https://github.com/solana-developers/solana-2048/blob/main/program/programs/lumberjack/src/instructions/push_in_direction.rs) · [Program on Explorer](https://explorer.solana.com/address/2o48ieM95rmHqMWC5B3tTX4DL7cLm4m1Kuwjay3keQSv?cluster=devnet)</p>

</div>
</div>

team. It's an example game: the program isn't audited.

## Checkout: a customer pays 1 USDC

<div className="sol-inaction not-prose">
<div className="sol-inaction-app">

<CheckoutDemo />

<p className="sol-inaction-note">Press **Run** to step through the payment, then the shop's check. It's test USDC on a local network, so no real money moves.</p>

</div>
<div className="sol-inaction-explain">

<p className="sol-inaction-kicker">What Solana is doing</p>

<div className="sol-inaction-flow" aria-hidden="true">
<span>Pay</span><i />
<span>Transaction</span><i />
<span className="is-code">Token program</span><i />
<span>Shop's USDC account</span>
</div>

<ol className="sol-inaction-steps">
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The order becomes one transfer</p>
<p>The customer's wallet signs a single USDC transfer of 1,000,000 base units (USDC has 6 decimals, so that is 1 USDC). It also carries a fresh `reference` address that ties the payment to this order.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The Token program moves the USDC</p>
<p>The USDC moves from the customer's USDC account to the shop's USDC account, not between the wallets directly. The fee is 5,000 lamports (0.000005 SOL), paid by the customer's wallet.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The shop checks the network, not the customer</p>
<p>The shop's server looks the transaction up by its signature. The order counts as paid only if it succeeded, carries the order's reference, and moved exactly 1 USDC of the USDC mint into the shop's account.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p className="sol-inaction-links">Tutorial: [Build a checkout](/developers/docs/payments/checkout)</p>

</div>
</div>

## Paid API: 0.001 USDC per call

<div className="sol-inaction not-prose">
<div className="sol-inaction-app">

<PaywallDemo />

<p className="sol-inaction-note">Press **Run** to call the API without paying, then pay and call again. The client is a script with its own wallet of test USDC.</p>

</div>
<div className="sol-inaction-explain">

<p className="sol-inaction-kicker">What Solana is doing</p>

<div className="sol-inaction-flow" aria-hidden="true">
<span>HTTP 402</span><i />
<span>Signed transfer</span><i />
<span className="is-code">Token program</span><i />
<span>API's USDC account</span>
</div>

<ol className="sol-inaction-steps">
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The API names its price</p>
<p>The first request gets `402 Payment Required`, with the price (1000 base units, 0.001 USDC), the USDC mint, and the address to pay.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The client signs a transfer, but doesn't send it</p>
<p>The client signs a USDC transfer for exactly that amount and sends the same request again, with the signed transfer in the `X-PAYMENT` header. No person approves the call.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The server settles it onchain, then answers</p>
<p>The server checks the transfer pays the price, sends it to the network, and waits for it to confirm. Then it answers `200` with the forecast. The network fee is 5,000 lamports (0.000005 SOL).</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p className="sol-inaction-links">Tutorial: [Paywall an endpoint](/developers/docs/payments/paywall-api)</p>

</div>
</div>

## Restricted asset: approved holders only

<div className="sol-inaction not-prose">
<div className="sol-inaction-app">

<AssetDemo />

<p className="sol-inaction-note">Press **Run** to step through the Lisbon Solar Fund (LSF), a demo asset, on a local test network.</p>

</div>
<div className="sol-inaction-explain">

<p className="sol-inaction-kicker">What Solana is doing</p>

<div className="sol-inaction-flow" aria-hidden="true">
<span>Transfer</span><i />
<span>Transaction</span><i />
<span className="is-code">Token-2022 program</span><i />
<span>Approved account?</span>
</div>

<ol className="sol-inaction-steps">
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">Every account starts frozen</p>
<p>Each new LSF token account opens in the `Frozen` state. The issuer approves an investor by thawing their account.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The token enforces the rule</p>
<p>A transfer to an approved investor confirms. The same transfer to an account nobody approved fails with Token-2022 error 17, `Account is frozen`, and moves no tokens. The rule lives in the token, so it applies in every wallet and app.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The issuer keeps two more controls</p>
<p>A pause blocks every transfer (error 67) until the issuer resumes the asset. As permanent delegate, the issuer can also move tokens out of an account without the holder's signature, for a court order or lost tokens.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p className="sol-inaction-links">Tutorial: [Issue an asset](/developers/docs/assets/issue-asset)</p>

</div>
</div>

## Trade: delivery versus payment

<div className="sol-inaction not-prose">
<div className="sol-inaction-app">

<TradeDemo />

<p className="sol-inaction-note">Press **Run** to price the trade, try to settle it before the buyer pays, then settle it.</p>

</div>
<div className="sol-inaction-explain">

<p className="sol-inaction-kicker">What Solana is doing</p>

<div className="sol-inaction-flow" aria-hidden="true">
<span>Two escrows</span><i />
<span>SettleDvp</span><i />
<span className="is-code">DvP program</span><i />
<span>Both legs, or neither</span>
</div>

<ol className="sol-inaction-steps">
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">The price is fixed when the trade is created</p>
<p>The settlement agent reads the USDC/USD price from Pyth's price feed account, converts 4 LSF at \$1.25 a share into USDC, and records the terms with the DvP program.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">Each side funds its own escrow</p>
<p>The seller puts the 4 LSF in the trade's asset escrow, and the buyer puts the USDC in its cash escrow. LSF accounts start frozen, so the issuer first approves the asset escrow, as it would a new investor.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p className="sol-inaction-step-t">Settlement is all or nothing</p>
<p>With the cash leg missing, `SettleDvp` fails with DvP error 4 (leg not funded) and nothing moves. Once both escrows hold the agreed amounts, the same instruction moves the LSF to the buyer and the USDC to the seller in one transaction.</p>
</li>
</ol>

<p className="sol-inaction-links">Tutorial: [Settle a trade](/developers/docs/defi/settle-trade)</p>

</div>
</div>
