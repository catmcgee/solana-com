Confidential Balances is a Token-2022 extension that keeps transfer amounts and
balances encrypted on Solana, while the token account addresses stay public.
This video explains how it works and why it matters for payroll, trading, and
everyday payments. Pair it with the
[Confidential Balances guide](/developers/docs/tokens/extensions/confidential-transfer).
